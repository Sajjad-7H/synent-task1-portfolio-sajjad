02 — about me

I work at the point where language meets code — fine-tuning transformer ensembles to read Bangla, Banglish, and everything in between, then shipping the tools that put that research in front of real people.

My thesis combines BanglaBERT, MuRIL, and XLM-RoBERTa into a weighted ensemble for emotion detection across Bangla, Banglish, and code-mixed text — evaluated across 79 model runs and three stratified data splits, with a cross-domain analysis of how badly single-script training collapses on mixed input.

Outside the notebook, I build full-stack products end to end. StudyHub, a peer resource-sharing platform for my department, went from idea to a live deployment on Vercel and Render with role-based auth and Cloudinary storage. I like projects that force both halves of my brain to show up.
